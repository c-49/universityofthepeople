This assignment aims to assess your ability to reflect on and consider your own experiences with stress and your understanding of coping strategies. It also assess your ability to suggest approaches to enhance resilience in challenging circumstances.

Assignment

Stress tends to have a significant impact on our lives, both directly and indirectly. Consider the COVID 19 scenario to formulate the response.  One of the most noticeable outcomes during that period was a widespread increase in global stress levels. This stress was caused due to various factors, such as worries about getting infected, job insecurity, financial difficulties, adhering to pandemic-related restrictions, and so on.

For this assignment activity, please reflect on a time during the COVID-19 pandemic when you encountered stress, either directly or indirectly (whether it was when you or someone in your family got infected, health-related fears, economic challenges, or social issues).

In your reflection, kindly consider the following aspects:

Provide a brief overview of what happened during that specific time in your life.
Explain how stress affected your physical and mental health.
Share the coping strategies you employed at that time to effectively manage your stress. Consider problem-focused coping, emotion-focused coping, approach coping, and avoidance coping in this context.
End your discussion by suggesting two approaches for enhancing coping skills or building resilience among individuals, particularly during challenging circumstances like dealing with chronic illness.
Your Discussion should be a minimum of 500 words in length and not more than 700 words. Please include a word count. Following the APA standard, use references and in-text citations for the textbook and any other sources.
