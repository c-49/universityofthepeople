Grading criteria
Rubric
Explanation to selected health issue	
Provides a comprehensive and insightful introduction, capturing the significance and complexity of pain. Provides a compelling explanation of why pain is a health concern, demonstrating in-depth knowledge.
10 points
Provides a clear and coherent introduction to pain, establishing its relevance. Clearly explains why pain is a health concern, with appropriate depth and context.
8 points
Provides a basic introduction to pain that lacks a clear rationale. Includes a basic explanation of why pain is a health concern, but lacks depth.
5 points
Provides a vague or incomplete introduction to pain and/or the response fails to explain why pain is a health concern.
3 points
Did not provide any explanation to pain.
0 points
Factors affecting the experience of pain	
Identifies various strong and persuasive factors affecting the experience of pain. Demonstrates a strong understanding of the complexities and interplay of all relevant factors.
20 points
Identifies adequately reasoned factors affecting the experience of pain. Demonstrates a good and convincing understanding of the most relevant factors.
16 points
Identifies a limited number of factors affecting the experience of pain. Demonstrates a basic understanding of some relevant factors.
12 points
Identifies limited or unrelated factors affecting the experience of pain. Demonstrates a lack of understanding of relevant factors.
6 points
Did not explain any factors affecting the experience of pain.
0 points
Explanation of the Gate Control theory of Pain	
Accurately explains the theory with depth and clarity. Provides an exceptional and insightful explanation of key points, showcasing profound knowledge.
20 points
Accurately explains the theory with depth and clarity. Outlines key points of the theory comprehensively.
16 points
Explains the theory associated with pain but lacks clarity. Outlines some key points of the theory with limited clarity.
12 points
Mentions the theory but relates it vaguely or inaccurately with pain. Lacks clarity or missed out on outlining key points of the theory.
6 points
Did not provide any explanation to the theory.
0 points
Implementation and Real-World Example	
Provides comprehensive and insightful analysis regarding implementing the theory. Includes a real-world example that is exceptionally relevant and illustrative, showcasing an impressive grasp of how the theory can be effectively applied.
20 points
Provides thorough and adequate analysis regarding implementing the theory. Includes a real-world example that is relevant and clear, showcasing a good grasp of how the theory can be effectively applied.
16 points
Provides basic or vague analysis regarding implementing the theory. Includes a real-world example that lacks depth, showcasing a limited grasp of how the theory can be effectively applied.
12 points
Provides unclear or incomplete analysis regarding implementing the theory. Includes a real-world example that lacks depth and/ or real-world example is missing, showcasing a lack of grasp of how the theory can be effectively applied.
6 points
Did not provide analysis regarding the implementation of theory and the real-world example.
0 points
Approaches to Managing Pain	
Provides a comprehensive overview of various approaches to manage pain, with detailed explanations supported by current research and evidence, showcasing a profound understanding of their effectiveness.
10 points
Provides an adequate and convincing overview of some approaches to manage pain, with adequate explanations supported by current research and evidence, showcasing a good understanding of their effectiveness.
8 points
Provides a satisfactory and basic overview of a few approaches to manage pain, but the explanations may lack depth or relevance, showcasing a limited understanding of their effectiveness.
5 points
Provides a brief and vague overview of a few approaches to manage pain, with adequate but the explanation may be incomplete or unclear, showcasing a weak understanding of their effectiveness.
3 points
Did not provide any discussion on approaches to manage the health issue.
0 points
Sources and Evidence	
Demonstrates skillful use of high-quality, credible, relevant sources to develop ideas that are appropriate for the discipline and genre of the writing.
10 points
Demonstrates consistent use of credible, relevant sources to support ideas that are situated within the discipline and genre of the writing.
8 points
Demonstrates an attempt to use credible and/or relevant sources to support ideas that are appropriate for the discipline and genre of the writing.
5 points
Demonstrates an attempt to use sources to support ideas in writing.
3 points
Uses no sources to support ideas in writing.
0 points
Proper delivery to convey a clear message	
Uses graceful language that skillfully communicates meaning to readers with clarity and fluency and is error-free.
10 points
Uses straightforward language that conveys meaning to readers with clarity. The language has a few errors.
8 points
Uses language that conveys meaning to readers. The writing may include some errors.
5 points
Uses language that conveys meaning to readers. The writing may include some errors.
3 points
Uses language that most of the time impedes meaning because of errors in usage or distracts the readers.
0 points
