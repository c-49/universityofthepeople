Assignment

After going through unit-5 from the textbook and the article provided, please proceed to the assignment activity.

Consider yourself as a healthcare worker at a community health center. As a healthcare worker, you are tasked  to develop a program to help community members deal with chronic pain, like arthritis or pain from cancer treatment and so on.

Plan a program to cater to these community members and include the following in your program.

Provide a concise introduction to pain, explaining why it is a health concern.
Explore various factors that can affect people's experience of pain.
In your own words, explain the Gate Control theory of Pain, outlining its key points.
  Taking idea from Gate Control theory of Pain, discuss how you intend to implement it in your program by including a relevant real-world example.
Conclude by describing a few approaches to effectively manage pain.
