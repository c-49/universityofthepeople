This assignment will be assessed by your instructor using Discussion Forum Rubric Unit 6.

This assignment will assess your understanding and ability to connect psychological factors to the development and progression of chronic illness. It further assesses your knowledge of the impact of chronic illness on an individual's behavior and emotions and explores the role of psychology in the treatment and management of chronic illnesses.

Assignment

Think about someone you are familiar with who has been dealing with a chronic illness. Use this person's situation to create your response to this assignment. Alternatively, you can select any chronic illness you prefer if you don't have a personal connection to someone dealing with chronic illness. 

Respond to the following questions while keeping the chosen chronic illness in mind.

Explain how psychology is connected to the development and progression of chronic illness by discussing various factors, especially the psychological ones, in the person's life that might have contributed to the illness's development and progression.
Describe how the chronic illness has affected the person's behavior and psychological well-being.
Additionally, discuss the crucial role that psychology can play in effectively treating and managing this chronic illness by considering various treatment and management options available for it.
Your Discussion should be a minimum of 500 words in length and not more than 700 words. Please include a word count. Following the APA standard, use references and in-text citations for the textbook and any other sources.
