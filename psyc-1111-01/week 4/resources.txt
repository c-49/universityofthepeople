As you read through the learning resources and watch the videos consider the following:

 Think about how illness beliefs have shaped your understanding of the illnesses and adherence to treatment plans.
 Are your illness beliefs similar to your community, or different?
 Under what circumstances you might avoid seeking medical help? What are the reasons for your decision?
 What are some of the barriers in seeking timely healthcare and how can we remove them?
 What are the various factors that influence the health professional’s clinical decisions?

1. Dawkins, B., Renwick, C., Ensor, T., Shinkins, B., Jayne, D., & Meads, D. (2021). What factors affect patients' ability to access healthcare? An overview of systematic reviews.Tropical Medicine & International Health: TM & IH, 26(10), 1177–1188.  https://doi.org/10.1111/tmi.13651 or download PDF.

The article identifies a wide range of factors that can affect help-seeking behavior. These factors can be grouped into four main categories: individual-level factors, health system-level factors, community-level factors, and personal factors.
2. Keshavarzi, M. H., Safaie, S., Faghihi, S. A. A., & Zare, S. (2022). Barriers of physician-patient relationships inprofessionalism: A qualitative study. Journal of Advances in Medical Education & Professionalism, 10(3), 199–206. https://doi.org/10.30476/JAMP.2022.94010.1563 

The article explores the effective communication between health providers and patients and also its barriers through a qualitative approach.

Read Unit 4: Becoming Ill and the Role of Illness Cognitions, Help-Seeking, and Communication. (pages 37-51). This chapter delves into various aspects of individual’s illness beliefs, their decisions to seek help, and the role of health professionals in diagnostic and treatment decision-making.
