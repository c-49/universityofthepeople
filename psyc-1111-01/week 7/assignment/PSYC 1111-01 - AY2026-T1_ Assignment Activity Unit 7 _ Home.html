Assignment Activity Unit 7

Completion requirements
To do: Make a submission
Opened: Thursday, 16 October 2025, 12:05 AM
Due: Thursday, 23 October 2025, 11:55 PM


This assignment will assess your ability to recognize, appreciate, and address cultural influences on health.  Additionally, it will assess your ability to suggest strategies for delivering culturally competent care, which implies understanding and respecting the cultural diversity of your patient population.

Read the following and proceed with the assignment.

    • How culture influences health.
    • How culture influences health beliefs.

Assignment

As a healthcare professional working at a community health center, you've been tasked with preparing a document that examines the intricate relationship between culture and health within your community.

You can use your own cultural background or your experiences in the community where you currently live to create your report. Your report should include the following elements:

    • Explain the role of culture, especially cultural beliefs about health, in influencing individuals' health-related behaviors and their likelihood to seek medical help.
    • Identify some cultural beliefs, particularly those related to health, that are present or observed within this community or culture.
    • By using your understanding of how culture affects health, suggest some strategies for delivering culturally competent care to patients from various cultural backgrounds.

Instructions for submission

    • The document should be a minimum of 500 words and not more than 750 words not including the reference list. Please include a word count.
    • Follow the APA standard, use references and in-text citations if you use ideas from the readings or other sources. Refer to the UoPeople APA Tutorials in the LRC for help with APA citations.
    • Follow proper grammar and sentence structure. Use proper delivery to convey your message. Edit for spelling and grammar errors.

This assignment will be assessed by your instructor using the rubric below.
