Grading criteria
Rubric
Description of Chosen Framework or Theoretical Approach	
Provides a comprehensive and insightful description of the chosen framework or theory that would be targeted in the intervention. The rationale for why it has the potential for behavior change is well-elaborated.
20 points
Provides a clear and coherent description of the chosen framework or theory that would be targeted in the intervention. The rationale for why it has the potential for behavior change is mostly adequate and clear.
16 points
Provides a basic description of the chosen framework or theory that would be targeted in the intervention. The rationale for why it has the potential for behavior change is limited, and lacking in detail or specificity.
12 points
Provides a vague or incomplete description of the chosen framework or theory that would be targeted in the intervention. The rationale for why it has the potential for behavior change lacks clarity, and accuracy or is either minimal or absent.
6 points
Did not provide any description of the chosen framework or theoretical approach
0 points
Applying the Chosen Theory to Unhealthy Behavior	
Provides a strong and persuasive explanation that demonstrates how the chosen theory be applied to the selected unhealthy behavior. The response offers a deep understanding of the model's applicability and its use in creating an effective intervention.
30 points
Provides an adequately reasoned explanation that demonstrates how the chosen theory be applied to the selected unhealthy behavior. The response offers a good understanding of the model's applicability and its use in creating an effective intervention.
23 points
Provides some reasoning that demonstrates how the chosen theory be applied to the selected unhealthy behavior. The response offers a basic or satisfactory understanding of the model's applicability and its use in creating an effective intervention.
16 points
Provides limited or vague reasoning that demonstrates how the chosen theory be applied to the selected unhealthy behavior. The response may lack a basic understanding making it difficult to understand how the theory would be used for intervention.
9 points
Did not provide any explanation regarding applying the chosen theory to unhealthy behavior
0 points
Strategies for promoting public health outcomes.	
Provides a strong and persuasive analysis of various strategies for promoting public health outcomes. Clearly prioritizes a strategy and justifies the choice with a well-structured argument, taking into account effectiveness, feasibility, and potential impact on behavior change.
30 points
Provides an adequately reasoned analysis of different strategies for promoting public health outcomes. Prioritizes a strategy and provides a well-reasoned explanation for the choice. Demonstrates a good understanding of the factors influencing behavior change and how the chosen strategy aligns with these factors.
23 points
Provides some basic analysis of different strategies for promoting public health outcomes. Prioritizes a strategy and provides some reasoning for the choice, although the explanation might lack depth or clear connection to behavior change factors.
16 points
Provides a limited or vague response regarding various strategies to promote public health outcomes. The discussion of potential strategies may lack clarity or accuracy. The prioritized strategy might be mentioned without sufficient explanation or rationale, and its connection to behavior change may be unclear
9 points
Does not explain any strategies for promoting public health outcomes.
0 points
Sources and Evidence	
Demonstrates skillful use of high-quality, credible, relevant sources to develop ideas that are appropriate for the discipline and genre of the writing.
10 points
Demonstrates consistent use of credible, relevant sources to support ideas that are situated within the discipline and genre of the writing.
8 points
Demonstrates an attempt to use credible and/or relevant sources to support ideas that are appropriate for the discipline and genre of the writing.
5 points
Demonstrates an attempt to use sources to support ideas in writing. Errors do not impede meaning nor overly distract the reader.
3 points
Uses no sources to support ideas in writing.
0 points
Proper delivery to convey a clear message	
Uses graceful language that skillfully communicates meaning to readers with clarity and fluency and is error-free.
10 points
Uses straightforward language that conveys meaning to readers with clarity. The language has a few errors.
8 points
Uses language that conveys meaning to readers. The writing may include some errors.
5 points
Uses language that sometimes impedes meaning because of errors in usage. Errors do not impede meaning nor overly distract the reader.
3 points
Uses language that most of the time impedes meaning because of errors in usage or distracts the readers.
0 points
