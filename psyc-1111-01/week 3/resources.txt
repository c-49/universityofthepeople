As you read through the learning resource and watch the video consider the following:

What are various theoretical frameworks related to behavior change?
In what manners do the various theories about behavior change demonstrate differences or display similarities?
What are various strategies or tools that could be used for behavior change?
How are the strategies or tools for behavior change similar, and how are they different?
Read


1. Michie, S., Van Stralen, M. M., & West, R. (2011). The behaviour change wheel: A new method for characterising and designing behaviour change interventions. Implementation science, 6, 42. https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-6-42

The article introduces an overview of the COM-B model and elaborates Behavior Change Wheel (BCW), which is a framework for conceptualizing and designing behavior change interventions. 
2. Ngigi, S., & Busolo, D. N. (2018). Behaviour change communication in health promotion: Appropriate practices and promising approaches. International Journal of Innovative Research and Development, 7(9), 84-93. https://doi.org/10.24940/ijird/2018/v7/i9/sep18027

The article emphasizes the power of communication as a tool for promoting favorable health behaviors to prevent diseases. It delves into communication models that can guide the creation of strategies fostering protective habits, reducing risk behaviors, and promoting positive actions.
3. Ogden, J. (2019). The psychology of health and Illness: An open access course.McGraw Hill. The psychology of health and illness: An open access course [PDF], licensed under CC BY 4.0

Read Unit 3: Behavior Change, Pg. 29- 36. This unit provides a comprehensive overview of how behavior change can be facilitated using four main theoretical perspectives. It highlights the diverse range of strategies available to psychologists and healthcare professionals for guiding individuals toward healthier behaviors.
