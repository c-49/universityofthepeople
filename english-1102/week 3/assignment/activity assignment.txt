This assignment will assess your knowledge and skills about:

Demonstrating the ability to analyze and critically evaluate academic sources.
Applying paraphrasing techniques to avoid plagiarism.
Demonstrating the ability to synthesize information from various sources.
Locating gaps in review of existing literature.

Assignment
Based on the five credible sources from the previous unit, prepare a summary of each source. Remember to quote and also paraphrase.

Next, write one or two paragraphs, synthesizing the information from various sources.

Finally, discuss the gaps in existing literature and how you plan to address the gaps. If there is no gap, discuss what innovation you wish to bring to your paper.


Submission Instructions
Read the rubric on how your assignment will be graded.  
Your assignment should be:
At least 500 words and not more than 750 words (not including the reference list or the title).
Double-spaced in Times New Roman font, which is no greater than 12 points.
Support your arguments with sources and evidence.
Use high-quality, credible, relevant sources to develop ideas appropriate for the discipline and genre of writing.  Explore additional details on the INFORMATION LITERACY page (located on the right-hand side of this page).
