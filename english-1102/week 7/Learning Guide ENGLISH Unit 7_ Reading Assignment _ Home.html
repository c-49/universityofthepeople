Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings, consider the following:

  1. What is an abstract?

  2. What does one include (and omit) in an abstract?

Read:

1. Serdikoff, S. (2021). Research Methods for the Behavioral Sciences [PDF]. (5th Edition). GALILEO Open Learning Materials. https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/psychology-textbooks/12/

  • Read Chapter 3: Overview of the Scientific Process, pages 121-122 (as per numbers in the book)
    These two introductory pages provide an example of an abstract.

2. Writing an abstract for your research paper. (n.d.). The Writing Center. https://writing.wisc.edu/handbook/assignments/writing-an-abstract-for-your-research-paper/

  • This webpage offers insightful information on elements of an abstract, with clear examples.
