This assignment will assess your knowledge and skills about:

Organizing and present a coherent narrative that integrates multiple perspectives.
Refining research objectives and questions based on literature analysis.

Assignment
Based on literature review of the sources and identification of gap in the previous two units, craft a refined problem statement. The problem statement should identify a clear research gap and articulate the significance of addressing this gap.

Next, draft well-defined research objectives and questions. Ensure they are aligned with the problem statement and demonstrate critical analysis of relevant literature.

Recommended structure:
Problem Statement:
Describe the issue or gap in knowledge.
Justify the importance of addressing this problem.
Research Objectives:
Define the overall goals of the research.
These should be concise and directly address the problem statement.
Research Questions:
Frame specific inquiries that will help achieve the objectives.
Ensure they are clear, focused, and answerable through research.
Link each question to a specific objective.
