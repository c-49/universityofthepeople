This methodology will enable me to gather data from a substantial number of participants and use statistical tests to identify meaningful relationships.
Finally, the structured nature of quantitative research ensures consistency in data collection and allows for replication, which strengthens the reliability of findings. Using standardized measurement tools will help minimize bias and provide clear, objective results.
