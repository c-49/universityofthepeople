This assignment will assess your skills and knowledge about:

Identifying and describe qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods research approaches.
Determining the appropriate research methodology for their study and justify their choice.
Drafting e-mails.

Assignment
Based on the readings of this unit and your own understanding, answer the following discussion questions:

Draft a professional email to a potential advisor or collaborator, explaining your research idea and seeking their feedback or guidance. Focus on clarity, tone and conciseness. In your email, identify which research methodology (qualitative, quantitative or mixed-methods) would best suit your study. Discuss why this approach is most appropriate for the research goals.


Submission Instructions
Your discussion should be between 200 - 350 words in length (not including the reference list).
You may use the course readings or any relevant source to respond to the questions and your peer replies.
Remember to locate, use, cite, and list references in all your discussion posts to validate statements of fact or corroborate personal opinions/points of view.
Refer to the UoPeople APA Tutorials in the LRC for help with APA citations. You can contact the librarian at library@uopeople.edu if you need help. You can also ask the expert in the Learning Resource Center Course: Learning Resource Center.

NOTE: Remember to post as early as possible. The first posting is due by Sunday and the reply is due by Wednesday.
Discussion Responses
Please post your initial response by Sunday and respond to at least two peers by providing comments or questions about their main post by Wednesday. Feedback should be appropriate, meaningful, and helpful. Overall, your comments should contribute positively to the conversation by broadening or clarifying it. Feedback should be in complete sentences. Read 'Peer feedback for students' to know more about how to give feedback.
